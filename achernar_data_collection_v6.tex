\section{Scope, priorities and standing decisions}
The purpose is to minimize wasted acquisition while preserving honest
scientific and operational evaluation. Begin with one actual camera/optical
configuration and the modes intended for use. A supported one-rig prototype
does not need a rented second rig; an unseen-rig claim does.

The original FQE 20/60/150-run tiers, COC thousands-per-class collection,
TED 3,600-frame campaign, five-body calibration inventory and 10,000 local
plate-solved-frame adapter trigger are no longer required starting conditions.
They were planning envelopes, not empirical sample-size proofs. Their
replacement is a sequence of small, decision-driven experiments.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Freeze the task, intended use, supported inputs and a baseline.
\item Inspect a small real payload and its rights/metadata before bulk intake.
\item Reproduce classical measurements and synthetic stress tests.
\item Run one public-trained candidate where it addresses a measured need.
\item Acquire/adjudicate only local development and independent evaluation gaps.
\item Check deployment parity, then observe in shadow mode before considering
any additional control authority.
\end{enumerate}

Native AstroM3 catalog research can proceed independently. FOP, learned
exposure/filter/guiding policies and AstroM3Edge do not block useful cloud,
focus-width, background or trailing measurements. No deadline or trained-model
count is an acceptance criterion.

\subsection{What remains authoritative}
Code and migrations determine executable behavior. V6 defines proposed data
contracts and decisions, not implemented ingestion or trained models. Classical
HFR controls current autofocus; neural FQE is shadow output. BGE and TED retain
classical baselines. Model lifecycle, integrity, runtime qualification,
activation and controller authority remain separate.

No new hardware purchases, rentals, cloud forecasts or dedicated multimodal
field campaigns are justified solely by this plan. A public source is admitted
only for the task and intended use its evidence supports.

\subsection{Admission checklist and transfer caps}
For imaging archives, start with metadata for 10--20 exposures from 3--5
sessions where available, and a proposed 2 GB total pilot transfer cap. The cap
bounds effort, not training adequacy. Choose a smaller individual-product
route when one archive bundle exceeds it. Do not expand solely to fill a row
counter. Full cloud logits, the year of MILAN, a multi-year Rubin database and
100 TB MMU are not initial requirements.

Before admitting each product, record: exact release/revision and URL;
license for data/code/weights separately; checksum; instrument/site/session;
HDU or array meaning; native units and scale; masks/variance; processing state;
CFA/black/white/clipping evidence; timestamp reliability; duplicate ancestors;
and available labels with their actual meaning. If clipping cannot be
established, exclude it from strict FQE labels while optionally retaining an
explicit robustness-only example.

A suitable image is not automatically a suitable outcome label. A processed
variance map is an independent pipeline estimate, not measured sensor noise
truth. A PSF model is a fitted source, not an independent exposure. A saturated
or cloudy archive frame is useful for failure behavior only when its validity
and label evidence are retained.

\subsection{Rights and reproducibility}
Lenghu has an Apache-2.0 dataset tag; teacher/backbone terms are separate.
SWIN's original hosts link CC BY-NC 4.0. MILAN/DeepSpaceYolo are CC BY-NC-ND;
HSC archive terms are noncommercial scientific/educational; LCO states CC BY-NC
2.0. AstroM3 source, processed-data listings and pretrained checkpoints have
different terms; MMU preserves subset restrictions. A source-code license does
not settle a downloaded database or user-log license.

Maintain a separately identifiable research lineage and an intended-use-cleared
lineage. Retain permissions, citations and downstream source ancestry. This
plan makes no blanket legal assertion about whether a particular weight is an
adaptation. Additional permission may be recorded later without silently
rewriting the provenance of an existing artifact.

For FQE retain \code{log(HFR_px)}, uncertainty and accepted-source coverage.
Position is provenance, not a pixels-only FQE input. Disable real within-run
ranking on unrelated archive exposures. Maintain a separate simulated-sequence
flag if ranking synthetic curves.

For TED retain latent trail length/axial angle, noiseless reference shape and
noisy estimated ellipticity separately. Angle is modulo 180 degrees; almost
round sources need an undefined/low-confidence angle, not arbitrary direction.
Single-crop shape cannot identify tracking versus coma or wind. Use WCS before
claiming sky-coordinate RA/Dec axes.

\subsection{Split hierarchy and adaptive collection}
All derivatives of a source exposure or latent scene family stay together;
all modalities/repeated observations of an object stay together. Split before
augmentation. FQE/FOP runs are nested within nights and rigs; use the coarser
level needed for the claim. COC needs whole-night/weather-episode/camera-era
separation. Similar sky frames separated by minutes are not independent
conditions. Unknown dates require conservative group families.

Use development data for transforms and training; independent validation for
selection/calibration; an untouched final set for a final claim. Never tune
thresholds on the final test. If its failures guide new training, record that
the test has been consumed and obtain fresh final evidence. Public benchmark
labels already seen during development are not an independent local test.

Report source exclusions, usable frame coverage and independent group counts.
A million crops are not a million observing outcomes. Confidence intervals
must respect night/run/episode clustering. Fix the failure event and tolerance
before choosing sample size. With zero failures in independent stationary
trials, the one-sided 95\% upper bound is $1-0.05^{1/n}$; 59 such trials are
needed for a bound below 5\%. This is an illustration, not a demand for 59
adjacent images or an assertion that 59 sessions settle every use.

\section{Compact local runbooks}
\subsection{RB-A: adjudicate existing clouds before collecting more}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Preserve original CR3s and existing provisional ledgers. Choose one
reproducible RAW/preview transform, crop/sky mask and deployed resolution.
\item Review a diverse panel from all capture groups, including star-bearing
openings, soft/low-signal images, foreground and bright/transient contamination.
Compare native detail with the actual model input; do not label from a view
the model cannot see without recording that difference.
\item Record cloud presence/region, visible-sky coverage if supportable,
attenuation evidence, contamination and validity separately. Preserve legacy
0--4 candidates and alternates; leave ambiguity unknown. No-stars alone is
not overcast. Nearby weather is context, not a substitute for frame truth.
\item Adjudicate useful existing examples before new photographs. Keep uncertain
clock/session groups conservative; never infer exact UTC from file mtime.
\item Freeze development and independent prospective-test roles before local
adaptation. The current burst collection does not establish all five classes.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{RB-B: targeted cloud transfer episodes}
Only start after RB-A and a source-trained inference/input check name a gap.
Fix camera clock/timezone, lens/pointing, manual focus, exposure recipe and RAW
preservation. Log site, true time anchor, field/sky mask, camera settings,
moon/illumination when computable, and observer evidence.

For a first tranche, cap effort at approximately 3--5 additional independent
nights or weather episodes, not a compulsory campaign. Prioritize clear versus
thin/star-attenuation transitions and one missing glow/obstruction contrast.
A regular sparse cadence such as 30--60 seconds can preserve transitions;
choose approximately 50--150 diverse keyframes per night after thinning if that
many add information. These are workload bounds, not independence assumptions.
Record short cloud transitions contemporaneously rather than spreading one
10-minute label across a changing field. Independent reference photos can
help adjudicate but do not measure optical depth automatically.

Keep at least one untouched episode for each condition claimed and separate
validation/calibration groups. If a condition appears in only one episode,
report exploratory transfer rather than an independent performance estimate.
Stop an uninformative uniform burst; further examples should answer a named
error or uncertainty. Do not deliberately expose equipment to rain or damage
to manufacture a challenge case; existing invalids and safe simulations suffice.

\subsection{RB-C: supported-mode camera characterization}
Start with one actual acquisition mode/ISO/gain and a locked decoder version.
Record camera, lens/optics, firmware, compression/bit depth, sensor temperature
when measured, exposure, CFA/black/white metadata and processing state. For a
JPEG/video mode, characterize that transformed path separately; native RAW
constants do not automatically describe it.

A proposed small bench starting budget is roughly 20 shortest dark/bias-like
RAWs and 8--12 stable illumination levels with at least a pair at each level.
Use paired differences to separate fixed spatial structure from temporal
variance; record means after appropriate black correction and inspect clipping
and nonlinearity. More repeats are justified if pair consistency or confidence
is poor. Cameras without a true bias mode need a valid short-dark protocol;
compressed SV105 video is not interchangeable with linear sensor RAW.

Characterize dark behavior only over exposure/temperature regimes intended for
use; ordinary imaging darks can supply it passively. Do not build every ISO and
exposure library before a task requires it. Flat-field calibration for science
and PTC illumination measurements are related but distinct products. Preserve
all originals and units. Document gain/read-noise fit assumptions and residuals;
a single mean--variance slope is not proof of ideal Poisson behavior.

The gate is reproducible black/clipping/linearity evidence and a calibration
bundle for the supported mode. Unknown values remain unknown; drop strict
labels dependent on missing thresholds. Expand modes only when enabled in the
product or a measured error points to the missing characterization.

\subsection{RB-D: compact focus-width and outcome experiment}
Confirm safe travel/focus reach and acquisition first; this plan does not
assert the R50 reaches prime focus on the Z114. Use a configuration that actually
produces resolved, unsaturated stars. Reuse any ordinary verified autofocus
runs. If manual focus lacks readable position, record ordered settings for
width research; do not claim signed-step FOP labels or autonomous verification.

Begin with a 6--10-run effort cap over approximately 2--3 distinct sessions
when available. This can diagnose transfer, not qualify a general release.
Use enough positions on both sides of an interior minimum, dense near focus;
a starting 9--15 positions with 2--3 repeated frames can reveal scatter before
expanding to the historical 21-by-5 procedure. Adjust sampling to actual curve
resolution and repeatability, not a universal step count.

For each sample log capture/position/move times, actual readable position and
validity, direction/reversal, settling, settings, temperature provenance,
source/asset checksum and disturbances. Keep central and edge stars; retain
rejection/coverage statistics. Record failed/aborted runs without fake optima.
An interior fitted optimum needs uncertainty and independent final-image
verification after the commanded move; then log the observed result.

Compare classical and neural estimates on matched accepted stars and full-frame
coverage. Use held-out run/night groups for ranking, residuals and post-move
outcome. No focus improvement means no expansion merely to train a larger net.
Real control claims require reliable motor/sign/scale/backlash and actual final
outcomes; external optical defocus in wavelengths cannot supply those steps.

\subsection{RB-E: compact trailing/confounder experiment}
Use the same useful local sessions where possible. Select central and edge
stars; include short near-round exposures and denser steps where measured
ellipticity approaches the existing ambiguous band. An initial one-session
ladder across 2--3 fields and 6--10 exposure durations with a few repeats is a
planning cap; record actual durations and source coverage.

An ideal untracked sidereal displacement is approximately
$15.041\cos\delta$ arcsec per second, converted through local image geometry.
It does not exactly label observed ellipticity or identify wind versus tracking.
Plate scale/WCS or another measured angular calibration is required for physical
units; image-coordinate shape benchmarks can proceed without pretending those
units are known. Alt-az tracking retains field rotation.

Retain coma, defocus, saturation, blends, weak stars and real wind/disturbance
flags as confounders. A windy session is an optional gap-filling observation,
not a compulsory night. Compare estimated shape/axial angle and frame false
alerts with the classical baseline. Round/low-SNR angles can be undefined. No
neural benefit on the measured ambiguous cases means stop the CNN branch.

\subsection{RB-F: passive thermal and decision logging}
During necessary autofocus, record verified best position, ambient temperature
and probe/time quality, optical train/filter, resets and actual move success.
The current FDF state is position, independent drift rate and thermal
coefficient. A monotonic temperature/time trajectory can confound the last two;
seek independent cooling/warming behavior only if identification or forecasts
remain poor. Do not prescribe a fixed degrees-C span as universally sufficient.
Hold out nights and compare against recent-best/no-drift baselines and the
rig's measured critical-focus threshold.

For exposure/filter/guiding decisions retain predecision context and measurement
ages; safe/available actions; policy/version; requested/chosen/executed action;
probabilities when randomized; actual settings/ownership/overrides; frame/event
lineage; realized reward components and attribution window; and interruptions.
Do not invent propensities for deterministic past choices. A fitted after-the-fact
propensity model does not create randomization or remove hidden confounding.
Passive fixed-policy data is useful for simulator checks without claiming
unbiased new-policy evaluation. No bandit exploration starts merely to fill a
training table.

\section{Stop, expand or proceed: acceptance gates}
The matched comparisons and acquisition decision ledger are detailed in
\pathref{docs/public-dataset-audit-2026-10-12/experiment-design.md}.
For COC compare the current heuristic, public-human-only candidate and the same
candidate with disjoint local adaptation on identical validation groups. For
FQE compare classical measurement with one architecture under synthetic,
approved public-real and mixed training. Do not enlarge architecture and data
simultaneously and then attribute the result to a dataset. TED/BGE first test
whether deterministic source/mask/background improvements resolve the error.

Every additional tranche must name a gap, explain why existing/public/synthetic
evidence cannot answer it, specify the comparison it will decide, freeze its
group/partition role, and state an effort cap and next decision. Data-efficiency
curves use nested training groups and fixed validation groups. Do not repeatedly
peek at a final test or stop a reliability test at its first favorable result.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{0.23\linewidth}L{0.34\linewidth}L{0.34\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule Gate & Stop or resolve first & Evidence allowing the next stage\\\midrule\endhead
Intake & Unclear rights, units, labels, ancestors or clipping for strict labels &
Small actual payload, reviewed metadata/terms and correct provenance.\\
Baseline & No reproducible classical measurement or reference semantics &
Known-unit benchmark, failures/coverage and independent synthetic checks.\\
Candidate value & Only teacher agreement or simulator success; no operational
need identified & Improved relevant error/coverage/latency tradeoff on frozen
independent development/validation groups.\\
Local transfer & Missing clear/thin or mode parity; correlated-only test &
Named gap filled and separate independent target-domain evidence.\\
Deployment & Unmatched preprocessing/export, uncalibrated uncertainty or
unqualified runtime & Golden input/output parity, calibrated held-out behavior,
resource qualification and inspectable shadow logs.\\
Control & Unknown motion, unbounded action, no attributable outcomes or support &
Controller-specific real outcome evidence and existing safety/ownership gates;
artifact selection alone never grants authority.\\
Expansion & Bigger corpus/rig list with no named decision benefit &
Residual slice, missing supported condition or confidence interval justifies
specific new data; freeze its role before collection.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

For COC report local recall at a predeclared false-pause constraint, thin-cloud
misses, contamination/unknown behavior and calibration; masks and whole-frame
decisions need separate metrics. For FQE report accepted-cohort log-HFR error,
uncertainty and full-frame coverage plus real focus outcomes. TED needs
ellipticity/axial-angle errors and frame false alerts, including confounders.
BGE needs calibrated unit/residual/gradient/crowding checks. FOP/FDF require
actual convergence/forecast outcomes; policy learners require defensible policy
comparison. Native TME needs held-out-object retrieval/missing-modality tests,
not only matching embedding dimensions.

\section{Additional algorithms and deliberate deferrals}
FW-1 needs calibrated per-filter response, not a new generic ML corpus. FW-2
is a policy with action/outcome requirements. FW-3 begins with explicit
constraints/objectives and deterministic optimization scenarios. M-1/M-2 need
actual guide/dither commands and attributable settle/science outcomes. M-3
needs genuine solve-failure/recovery need, not a low-star-count proxy. M-4 needs
this mount's solved residuals over held-out sky coverage, coordinates and
mount/pier configuration. Public logs and simulated fields help software tests;
they do not supply the missing local labels or usage rights.

Do not start a separate campaign for any of these before supported hardware,
truthful telemetry and a fixed baseline exist. Harvest during ordinary use.
AstroM3Edge is deferred until object IDs, valid stellar matches, identifiable
single-image information and an explicit alignment target exist. Neither a
10,000-frame quota nor a large galaxy archive answers those questions.

The follow-up AI synthesis supplies useful three-workstream framing and
shared/per-rig separation. Its asserted counts, fixed session suggestions and
simplified lifecycle are not adopted as evidence. V6 preserves explicit
scientific units and v5's orthogonal approval/qualification/authority states.
The historical manual procedures remain available when additional sampling is
justified; obsolete quotas or defects must not silently become current gates.

This is a plan and research result. Training, adapter implementation, camera
calibration and hardware qualification are outstanding work. The first useful
result is a small trustworthy experiment and a decision about what to retain,
change or collect next.
